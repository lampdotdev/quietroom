# Where The Little House's sounds come from

Every recording in WebApp/Web/sounds/ is a CC0 1.0 sound from Freesound (LICENSE-CC0-1.0.txt), credited in
credits.json. None of Moodist's own recordings are used: Moodist's README puts them under the Pixabay Content License
or CC0 without saying which file is which.

The noises (white, pink, brown) and the binaural beats are made in the page as it plays (Web/generators.js), not
recorded. The binaural beats use Moodist's presets (a 100 Hz tone with beats of 2, 5, 10, 20 and 40 Hz) and its way of
making them: two tones, one in each ear (MIT, Licenses/remvze/moodist/LICENSE).
